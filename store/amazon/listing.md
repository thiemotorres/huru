## Texts

**Title:** Huru – IPTV Player

**Short description:**
A clean, ad-free IPTV player for your own Xtream Codes subscription. Huru provides no channels or content.

**Long description:**
Huru is a calm, fast and ad-free player for IPTV services that use the Xtream Codes API. Sign in with the details from your own provider and watch live TV, movies and series – built for the Fire TV remote from the ground up.

• Live TV with a programme guide right in the channel list
• Fast zapping with Up/Down and an info overlay
• Starts straight into the last channel you watched
• Favourites and hideable categories
• Movies and series with search, "Continue watching" and automatic next episode
• No ads, no tracking, no account

Important: Huru is only a player. It does not provide, sell or recommend any channels, playlists, movies or series. You need your own subscription from a provider that is licensed to distribute its content.

## Submission checklist

1. Amazon developer account (free) at developer.amazon.com.
2. Make the GitHub repository public (privacy policy link) – or host `PRIVACY.md` elsewhere.
3. Create the app → "Android" → upload the **Amazon** APK (`-Pstore=amazon`).
4. Device support: Fire TV devices only. Vega OS devices (e.g. Fire TV Stick 4K Select) cannot run Android apps and are excluded automatically.
5. Upload graphics and texts from this folder.
6. Reviewers need a way to test: provide working **test credentials** for a legal Xtream service in the "testing instructions" field – without them the review is likely to fail.
7. Expect scrutiny: Amazon actively removes piracy-related IPTV apps. Keep the description clear that Huru is only a player.
