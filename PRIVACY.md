# Privacy Policy – Huru

_Last updated: 2026-11-12_

Huru is an IPTV player that connects only to the Xtream Codes server **you** enter.

- **What is stored:** your server address, username and password, favourites, hidden categories, the last watched channel and playback positions. All of it is stored **only on your device** (app-private storage) and is deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **What is sent:** your credentials and requests go **only to the server you entered**, to load channel lists, programme guides and streams. Images such as channel logos and posters are loaded from the URLs that server provides.
- **What Huru does not do:** no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no crash reporting, no accounts, no servers of its own. Huru does not share any data with the developer or third parties.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/thiemotorres/huru/issues.
